# Commercial licensing

HearthBit's original code is publicly auditable under a noncommercial
source-available license. A separate written license is required for commercial
uses that are not permitted by the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.

Examples that should be reviewed for a commercial license include:

- selling HearthBit, a modified build or a device with HearthBit preinstalled;
- offering HearthBit as part of a paid service or commercial support package;
- incorporating HearthBit code into a proprietary commercial product;
- commercial deployment by an organization that is not covered by the
  noncommercial-organization permissions in PolyForm.

Public-safety, health, government, education, research and charitable uses may
already qualify as noncommercial purposes under PolyForm. Review the actual
[license](LICENSE); this summary does not replace it.

To request commercial terms, contact Wilmer Alzate Roman through the repository
owner's verified contact channel:
<https://github.com/waar19/HearthBit>.

A commercial agreement can define support, distribution, branding, warranties,
liability, security maintenance and contribution rights separately. No
commercial rights are granted until both parties sign that agreement.
